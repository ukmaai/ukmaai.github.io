UKMAAI

UK Multi-Agent & Agentic AI Community

A community for researchers and practitioners across UK universities and industry working on multi-agent systems and agentic AI. We share news, events, collaboration opportunities and jobs, and bring people together in person.

About

From a Turing interest group to an independent community.

UKMAAI grew out of the Multi-Agent Systems Interest Group at The Alan Turing Institute. Since 2020 the group's mailing list has connected researchers and practitioners and carried community news, events, collaboration opportunities and job postings. The group has organised two editions of the UK Multi-Agent Systems Symposium, in 2020 and 2025.

In October 2026 the group moved beyond the Institute and continues as the UK Multi-Agent & Agentic AI Community. The broader name reflects the growing interest in agentic AI while keeping our roots in multi-agent systems.

We welcome anyone working on or interested in topics such as multi-agent reinforcement learning, game theory and mechanism design, negotiation and cooperation, swarm and multi-robot systems, agent-based modelling, LLM-based agents, and the safety, verification and governance of multi-agent and agentic systems.

We are grateful to The Alan Turing Institute for sponsoring both symposia and supporting the community's operations over the years.

    UK Multi-Agent Systems Symposium

    Our symposium brings together UK-based university and industry labs with a significant focus on multi-agent systems.

    UK-MAS 2025

    19 March 2025 · King's College London, Strand

    An in-person symposium hosted by The Alan Turing Institute in collaboration with the King's Institute for Artificial Intelligence, with keynotes, panels, talks, open discussions and poster sessions.

    Keynote
    Iain Couzin (Max Planck Institute of Animal Behavior), "The Geometry of Multi-Agent Decision Making"
    Invited speakers
    Mirco Musolesi (UCL), Joel Leibo (Google DeepMind), Chris Hicks (The Alan Turing Institute), Subramanian Ramamoorthy (University of Edinburgh), Gareth Siret (Ocado Technology), Richard Everson (University of Exeter)
    Programme highlights
    Multi-agent research in the age of foundation models; a toolbox session on open-source libraries including EPyMARL, VMAS, BenchMARL, JaxMARL, Melting Pot, Concordia, Mava, Jumanji and OpenSpiel; applications in warehouse automation, cyber security, assistive autonomy and air traffic control.
    Organisers
    Stefano Albrecht, Lukas Schäfer, Rahul Savani, Edmond Awad, Matteo Bettini, Amanda Prorok, Yali Du, Richard Willis and Ruth Drysdale

    UK-MAS 2020

    24 February 2020 · The Alan Turing Institute, London

    The first symposium, a free cross-disciplinary workshop at the British Library with talks from academia and industry, discussion sessions and networking.

    Topics
    Negotiation, game theory, swarm robotics, autonomous driving and verification, among others.
    Outcomes
    The map of UK multi-agent systems labs and the community mailing list both started here.
    Organisers
    Led by Stefano Albrecht, hosted by the AI Programme at The Alan Turing Institute
